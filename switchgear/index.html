MANUFACTURING CAPABILITIES

Laser cutting, forming
and welding

Our capabilities include laser cutting, CNC punching, forming and laser welding for panels, stainless-steel housings and structural assemblies.

01 / SHEET-METAL PROCESSING

CNC laser cutting & forming

We cut and form panels, partitions and mounting plates to your drawings, including profiles, openings and fixing holes for assembly.

  • Laser cutting and CNC punching / forming
  • Panel cut-outs and repeated hole patterns
  • Fit-up checks before welding

02 / WELDED ASSEMBLIES

Laser welding for sheet-metal boxes

We laser-weld sheet-metal housings and structural parts, controlling joint fit-up and welding distortion to meet the drawing requirements.

  • Stainless-steel housings for gas-insulated switchgear
  • Welded bases, supports and structural assemblies
  • Joint preparation and weld inspection
